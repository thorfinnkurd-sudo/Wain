# Wain? Privacy Policy (TEMPLATE — review with a qualified professional before publishing)

This template describes what the current code does. It is not legal advice and makes no compliance claim.

**Location.** The app reads your device location only while it is open, to show your position, guide you and
find nearby data. Location history is not stored or uploaded by the app.

**Data sent to third-party services when you use a feature** (their own policies apply):
- Map style/tile provider you configure: map tile requests (area you view).
- Routing server: your start point (current location) and destination, when you request or recalculate a route.
- Nominatim geocoder: your search text, or tapped coordinates for address lookup.
- Overpass API: the map area you view (places, speed cameras, bumps) and a small area around you (speed-limit tags).
- Shared reports backend (only if configured): report type, coordinates, optional comment, time, and a random
  per-install device ID used for rate limiting. No name, phone number or account is collected.

**Stored on your device.** Saved places, recent searches, settings, a random device ID, and (when no shared
backend is configured) your reports. You can delete recents, saved places and local reports in Settings.

**Not collected.** Contacts, microphone, advertising IDs, or analytics (none are implemented).
